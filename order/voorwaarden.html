Ordering and payment

The final price, including discount and delivery fee, is shown before placing the order. Online payments use Stripe. Cash is accepted at pick-up or on delivery. An order is first received and can then be accepted or declined by Grill Time. Online payments for declined orders are refunded to the original payment method.

Pick-up and delivery

Pick-up and delivery are available daily from 12:00 to 23:00. Pre-orders use the available time slots. We deliver only in Oostend within 4 km driving distance. Delivery costs €2.99 up to 2.5 km and €3.99 above 2.5 km up to 4 km. The minimum delivery order is €15 in food, excluding standalone drinks and delivery fees. Estimated delivery time is 25–45 minutes; the chosen slot and demand can affect actual timing.

Changes and questions

Call us promptly if you need to change an order. The statutory right of withdrawal generally does not apply to freshly prepared food. Your statutory consumer rights remain. Check allergen information before ordering.
